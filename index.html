How the numbers are made. Water remaining comes from the load cell: mass minus the specimen's dry mass and the fixings, as a fraction of the water present at the start. Mass is smoothed with a rolling median over ±35 min; handling windows are removed and the curve re-levelled across them, assuming the drying rate before the disturbance carries on. The projection fits an exponential (falling-rate) curve to the last 18 h. A drying front is logged when a probe falls 3 points below its own peak and stays there for at least 2 h. The mould index uses the VTT model's most sensitive (pine sapwood) class; gaps over 90 min are held, not interpolated. Above the fibre saturation point (≈28–30% moisture content) wood holds free water and the cavity reads close to 100% RH; below it, cavity RH tracks moisture content through the sorption isotherm.
