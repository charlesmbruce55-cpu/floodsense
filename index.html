Humidity inside the wall

The FloodSense signal: each probe reads near-saturated until the drying front reaches its depth

How it works

  • Two humidity probes sit in sealed holes at 15 mm and at mid-depth. While the brick around a probe is wet, its air stays near saturation.
  • A probe's drying front has arrived when its humidity drops 3 points below its starting level and stays there for 2 hours.
  • Water remaining comes from weighing the wall section every 10 minutes. Time to dry projects the recent falling-rate trend down to 5% water.
  • Mould risk uses the VTT mould-growth model (Hukka & Viitanen, 1999) on the shallow probe's humidity and temperature. M = 1 means microscopic growth.
